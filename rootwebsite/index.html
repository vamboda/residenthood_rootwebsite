Can I subscribe to Books, Sign, or another module on its own?

Under our current plan, Books, Sign, Pay, Docs, and Maintain require a ResidentHood Properties subscription. They are integrated modules within the platform and will not be offered as standalone subscriptions.

Can I use ResidentHood today?

Not yet. The platform is actively being developed. This website introduces our vision and planned capabilities; it is not a live application or an invitation to purchase.

Who is ResidentHood being built for?

Property managers, landlords, HOA managers, organization administrators, and the tenants and residents they serve.

When will the products launch?

We haven’t announced a launch date. We’re focused on building a strong foundation and will share availability here as plans take shape.
